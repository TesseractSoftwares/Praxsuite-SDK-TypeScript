# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique code, not people.

## What we expect

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their experience level, background or identity
- Give feedback on the work, with a reason attached
- Accept feedback on your own work the same way
- Say "I don't know" freely — it is more useful than a confident guess

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Publishing someone's private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is
representing this project.

## Reporting

Email **conduct@tesseractsoftwares.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential. We will respond within 5 business days.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that breach this
document. We would much rather have a conversation first.
